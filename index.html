How to Accurately Price Your Freelance Services

Setting hourly rates is one of the most critical decisions for freelancers, agencies, and independent consultants. Simply dividing your target salary by 2,000 working hours leads to severe underpricing because it ignores non-billable hours, self-employment tax liabilities, software overhead, and unpaid time off.

The Reverse-Engineering Formula

This calculator utilizes the standard financial reverse-engineering formula for independent operators:

Gross Revenue Required = (Net Income Target + Annual Expenses) / (1 - Tax Rate) * (1 + Profit Margin)

Why Non-Billable Time Matters

Unlike traditional employment where 100% of desk time is compensated, freelancers typically spend 30% to 40% of their working hours on administrative duties, sales calls, marketing, invoicing, and proposal writing. Accounting for your exact billable percentage ensures you remain profitable without burning out.
